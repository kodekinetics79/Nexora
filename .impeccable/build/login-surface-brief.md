# Login surface brief

## Purpose

Authenticate tenant users into Nexora while establishing, before credentials are entered, that the product governs the complete commercial chain from source evidence through cash collection.

## Audience

Sales representatives, sales managers, customer-service staff, procurement operators, operations staff, finance users, and authorized platform operators.

## Primary action

Sign in with an email address and password. If the same identity belongs to more than one tenant, choose the intended organization and continue.

## Product truth

The displayed ledger is explicitly illustrative. It demonstrates the implemented commercial spine: captured email, reconciled Lead, governed participation, Formal RFQ, order fulfilment, and payment application. It must never imply live customer data or promise functionality that is absent.

## Visual direction

Calm enterprise command ledger. A deep-navy evidence panel makes lineage and handoffs legible; a quiet, high-contrast authentication plane keeps sign-in dominant. Use Cambay for display headings and Source Sans 3 for operational text. Avoid glass effects, gradients, animated scanner motifs, excess cards, playful copy, and generic AI spectacle.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

All controls require accessible names, visible focus, persistent field borders, and at least 44px targets. Brand identity remains visible at tablet and mobile sizes. The ledger progressively condenses rather than causing horizontal scrolling. Reduced-motion preferences must be respected.

## Enterprise constraints

Preserve password-manager support, session-expiry notices, business-unit disambiguation, permission bootstrap, password recovery, and the separate platform-administration entry point. Authentication failure must never leave a half-established session.
